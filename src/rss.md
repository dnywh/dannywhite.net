The world is going mad one tweet at a time. We don't need those predatory platforms to keep up with eachother's work or ideas. A simpler, saner, technology has existed all along. That's RSS.

## What's RSS?

If you've listened to a podcast, you've used RSS. It stands for 'Really Simple Syndication' and is pretty much just that.

You can follow blogs, podcasts, or whatever else you like. When they publish something new, that new thing will appear quietly in your RSS reader.

No megacorp owns RSS. It's an open technology like the web or email. Here are a few resources for further reading:

- [About Feeds](https://aboutfeeds.com)
- [What is RSS](https://www.nps.gov/feeds/rss-help.htm)
- [RSS Specification](https://www.rssboard.org/rss-specification#whatIsRss)

## RSS readers

My favourite is [Reeder Classic](https://reeder.app/classic). [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com) is also nice. There are a bunch out there. Just try a few out and see what sticks.

It's a rainy day on Christmas break. You know what that means: a full-blown re-design and re-build of the website.

I'm calling it the "quilt", because that's the closest thing to the vision in my head. You can see the crude shakeout [here](http://quilt.dannywhite.net). Sorry about the mess.

The lesson I've taken away from past redesigns (often painfully) is to push-and-pull between the napkin sketch and reality. Otherwise you design something great that doesn't work in practice. Or you build something technically great but doesn't have a soul.

The quilt is currently in a frenetic back-and-forth between those two states. As it should be.
